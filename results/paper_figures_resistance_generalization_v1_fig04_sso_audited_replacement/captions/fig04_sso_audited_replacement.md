**Figure 4.** Audited waveform signatures associated with the configured 25-Hz SSO background in the archived 1,080-case corpus. (a,b) use documented execution-reference buses IEEE14 Bus 7 and IEEE30 Bus 30. They are execution/provenance labels; IEEE30 is not claimed as a physically validated PCC. (a) shows one-cycle (20-ms at the recorded 50-Hz fundamental) RMS-envelope deviation relative to the common No-SSO envelope, without per-condition normalization; yellow shading marks the IEEE30-dominant pre-event transient interval and is descriptive, not a modal-stability claim. (b) uses paired SSO-minus-No-SSO voltage differences to suppress the dominant fundamental waveform; dashed lines mark the configured 25-Hz target and the observed 75-Hz sideband. (c) reports difference-signal sinusoidal components at the selected three WMUs. All panels use matched 10-ohm SLG cases only for their pre-event interval (0.08–0.28 s); no new simulation was run.
